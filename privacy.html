Privacy Policy

4 Seasons: Gem Rivers
SupportPrivacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

4 Seasons: Gem Rivers is made by a solo developer. The game does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress, stars, crystals, settings and streaks are saved on your own device. If you're signed in to iCloud, Apple may sync them between your devices through your own iCloud account. The developer never sees them.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can turn Game Center off in your device's Settings.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information.

No tracking

There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and no data is sold or shared with anyone.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open a support request.
