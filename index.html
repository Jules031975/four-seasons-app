4 Seasons: Gem Rivers

Match gems, dig rivers, wake up the seasons
SupportPrivacy Policy

4 Seasons is a calm gem-matching puzzle game. Link gems of one colour to dig channels and lead the water to the goal through Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter and the Desert Oasis.

Getting help

Found a problem or have a question? Open a support request (a free GitHub account is needed) and it will be answered as soon as possible.

Common questions

How do I play? Drag across 3 or more matching gems that touch. They clear and dig the ground underneath. Reach the goal with the water before you run out of moves. Tap How to Play in Settings to see the guide again.

Can I take back a move? Yes. Tap Undo. The first undo in each level is free.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, it's also shared between your iPhone and iPad.

I bought something on another device. How do I get it back? Open the Crystal Shop (the crystal button on the map) and tap Restore Purchases. Crystals already spent can't be restored.

The sounds are too loud. Music and sound effects each have their own switch in Settings.

Can I make it easier to see? Settings has gem symbols for colour-blind players, larger text, and reduced motion.

Does it cost anything? The game is free to play. Optional in-app purchases are crystal packs, a one-time Starter Pack, and Unlock All Seasons. There are no ads.
